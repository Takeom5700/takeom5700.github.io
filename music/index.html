♪ questions I'm often asked

A few honest answers

Where are you in your search?
I began looking for a music teaching role in Southeast Asia this autumn, so my search is still at an early stage. I am open to any country in the region, and I would like to give priority, as far as I can, to the first school that reaches out to me.
Why do you want to become a music teacher now?
I enjoy my current work in AI consulting, and I now also support in-house training. Working with AI every day made one thing clear to me: work where the time spent together with people is meaningful in itself, like a school music or PE teacher, is the kind of work that will matter more, not less. Teaching music is also what I do best.
Why abroad, and why Southeast Asia?
I lived and worked in Kuala Lumpur for three and a half years, and I still return every year to perform. I feel happiest spending my days with people in Southeast Asia, and I would love to give my students that same warmth through music.
When did this idea begin?
During my last year in Kuala Lumpur. At the time I wasn't sure it was realistic. Back in Japan, joining an AI consulting firm and supporting training made me certain that this is the career I want to build.
Are you leaving your current career behind?
Not at all. This is a step forward, not an escape. I will bring what I have learned, including AI, training design and working across cultures, into the music classroom.
